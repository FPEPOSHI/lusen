Get a working request in under five minutes. Replace the placeholders with
whatever is true for your API.

## 1. Get a key

Say where a key comes from — a dashboard, an email to support, a console
command — and what a new account starts with.

## 2. Make your first call

Pick your simplest read endpoint. The reader should be able to paste this and
see real output.

```bash
curl 'https://api.example.com/api/ping' \
  -H 'Accept: application/json'
```

## 3. Handle the response

Show one successful body and one failure, so the reader knows what both look
like before they hit either.
